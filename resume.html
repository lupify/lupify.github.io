Los Alamos National Laboratory

Designed a multimodal machine learning pipeline for live kilonova event identification across survey telescope data streams, fusing heterogeneous observational channels for real-time detection of rare transients. The pipeline narrows the pool of candidate transients that can then be studied and characterized by mass, ejecta, and other collision properties.

2024 – 2025 Santa Clara, CA

AI Geophysical Researcher

Fujitsu Research of America, Converging Technologies Lab

Built AI frameworks for regional sea-surface emulation: autoregressive neural operator forecasting, physics-constrained generative downscaling, and stability strategies for long-horizon rollout. A novel spectral loss objective holds decadal rollouts stable and physically consistent at 8 km resolution without unphysical drift, and a coupled generative model downscales and bias-corrects to 4 km in the same step. Also contributed physics-informed neural operator wave forecasting and sparse data assimilation to SWR-Viz, an AI-assisted ship weather-routing and emissions analytics pipeline.
